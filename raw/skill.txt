# Acme Guides

Acme Guides is a small library of practical how-to guides at https://acme-guides.example/.

## How to use it

1. Get the list: `GET https://acme-guides.example/api/v1/guides` (JSON), or call the MCP tool `list_guides` at `https://acme-guides.example/mcp`.
2. Pick the guide that matches the user's question by its `title` and `summary`.
3. Walk the user through its `steps` one at a time. Confirm before anything that changes their accounts or devices.

## Notes

- Everything is public and read-only. No key needed.
- The whole site is also available as Markdown at https://acme-guides.example/index.md.
